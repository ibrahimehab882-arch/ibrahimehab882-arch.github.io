Privacy Policy

Outbreak Survivors · Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy explains how Outbreak Survivors handles your data. We do not run accounts, we do not ask for your name or email, and we never sell personal data.

Cloud Sync & Data Handling

To protect your progress and verify purchases, game progress (bio-capsules, upgrades, characters, missions, achievements, and purchase entitlements) is synced to a secure cloud database provided by Supabase.

This data is linked to an anonymous identifier generated for your device. We do not collect your name, email, or any other personal contact information. We use this data only to restore your progress if you reinstall the app and to prevent cheating.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded videos and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. Where required by law we ask for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the consent form in the app.

Rewarded videos are always opt-in. You can remove all forced interstitials permanently with the one-time “Remove Ads” purchase.

Google’s handling of ad data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing and verified by our secure server. We never receive or store your card details. We store a record of the purchase token and the entitlement in our cloud database to ensure your items are delivered and protected.

Analytics

We record anonymous gameplay events to balance the game. These events contain no personal identifiers and are not linked to your identity.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your Choices

You can reset or limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads), decline the in-app consent form, or buy “Remove Ads”. You can also clear all locally stored progress by clearing the app’s data.
