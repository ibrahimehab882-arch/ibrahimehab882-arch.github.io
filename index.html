Privacy Policy

Outbreak Survivors · Last updated 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game handles. We do not run accounts, we do not ask for your name or email, and we never sell personal data.

What stays on your device

Game progress (bio-capsules, upgrades, characters, missions, achievements, settings and purchase entitlements) is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it unless your platform backs it up.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded videos and, occasionally, interstitial ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data.

Where required by law we ask for your consent before the first ad request. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the privacy options form in the app settings. You can also reset or limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads).

Rewarded videos are always opt-in: nothing is unlocked and no reward is granted unless you choose to watch one.

You can permanently remove all forced interstitials with the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase; rewarded videos stay optional.

Google's handling of ad data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your card details. Google shares only the confirmation needed to unlock the item, and we store that entitlement locally so your items survive an app restart.

Analytics

We record anonymous gameplay events (for example: a run started or ended, an upgrade was bought) to balance the game. These events contain no personal identifiers and are not linked to your identity.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access.
  • No precise location data.
  • No user accounts, and no profile that could identify you.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can decline or change the in-app consent form at any time, reset or limit ad personalisation in your device settings, buy "Remove Ads" to stop forced interstitials, or clear all locally stored progress by clearing the app data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: onemovegames@gmail.com
